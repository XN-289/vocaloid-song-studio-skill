# 《未来已开机》封面提示词

> 用途：叙曲 App 作品封面 / 专题页展示图。不要求生成器画复杂文字（标题文字建议后期叠加或干脆不加）。

## 主方案（电影感插画）

```text
A cinematic illustration, night to dawn transition: a college dorm room at
around 5 AM. A student sits at the desk by the window with an acoustic guitar,
a phone lying on the desk with a small red recording dot glowing. Outside the
window, the sky is split: deep blue night on the left, first warm orange dawn
light on the right, one small satellite crossing the sky like a shooting star.
A potted green plant (pothos) on the windowsill. Warm lamp light inside the
room vs cool screen light on the student's face. Composition: viewed from
behind the student, window centered, plenty of negative space in the sky for
later title text. Color palette: deep blue, warm orange, soft white. No text,
no logos, no watermark.
```

## 备选方案（极简海报）

```text
Minimalist poster style: a single glowing power button symbol made of warm
sunlight, floating above a dark blue horizon line where the sea meets the sky
at dawn. Tiny silhouette of a student with raised hand pressing the "sunrise
button" from below. Flat design, deep blue background, warm orange gradient
light, lots of negative space, no text.
```

## 禁用元素

- 不出现具体真实人物面孔（防肖像争议）
- 不出现品牌 logo、手机型号标识
- 不要求画中文文字（生成器画字易乱码）
- 不做赛博朋克霓虹风（与本歌「温暖克制」的基调冲突）
