# 《月亮没有两岸》封面提示词

> 用途：叙曲 App 作品封面 / 专题页展示图。不要求生成器画复杂文字（标题文字建议后期叠加或干脆不加）。

## 主方案（中秋月夜·饭桌）

```text
A warm cinematic illustration, Mid-Autumn night: a full bright orange moon
hanging over a calm strait of sea. In the foreground, seen from a slightly
high angle, an old wooden family dinner table by a window: many dishes, and
one extra bowl with chopsticks placed neatly at an empty seat, the bowl still
steaming. A potted plant and a window frame on the right side, moonlight
falling across the table. Beyond the window, the sea glows under the moon,
tiny warm lights twinkling on the far shore. Color palette: deep night blue,
warm moon orange, soft wooden brown. Composition: moon upper center, table
lower third, plenty of negative space in the sky for later title text. No
text, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 备选方案（极简海报）

```text
Minimalist poster style: one round full moon split into two halves by a thin
horizontal line of sea waves, both halves still forming one complete glowing
circle. Below the horizon, a single small bowl with two chopsticks resting
across it, steam rising. Flat design, deep blue background, warm orange moon,
lots of negative space, no text.
```
