# 《往哪走》封面提示词

> 用途：叙曲 App 作品封面 / 专题页展示图。不要求生成器画复杂文字（标题文字建议后期叠加或干脆不加）。

## 主方案（路口·晨曦）

```text
A warm cinematic illustration: a young student with a backpack standing at a
crossroads on campus at dawn, seen from behind, slightly small in frame. The
road splits into several paths leading toward the horizon where the sun is
rising, warm orange light spilling over the street. Street signs at the fork
are blank, with no text. A soft morning fog, trees lining the roads. Color
palette: warm orange, soft blue, gentle gray. Composition: figure lower third,
roads converging to the sunrise in the upper center, negative space in the sky
for later title text. No text, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 备选方案（极简箭头）

```text
Minimalist poster style: a simple white arrow painted on the road splitting
into three arrowheads pointing toward a rising sun, drawn in flat style with
warm orange and deep blue, one small backpack silhouette standing at the split
point, plenty of negative space, no text.
```

## 禁用元素

- 不出现具体真实人物面孔（防肖像争议）
- 不出现校名、校徽、品牌 logo
- 路牌上不要画任何文字（生成器画字易乱码）
- 不做暗色调压抑风（本歌的底色是暖的）
