# 《别怕别怕》封面提示词

> 用途：叙曲 App 作品封面 / 专题页展示图。不要求生成器画复杂文字（标题文字建议后期叠加或干脆不加）。

## 主方案（清晨跑道·击掌）

```text
A bright warm cinematic illustration, campus morning: two young students on a
school running track at sunrise, seen from behind, one giving the other a
high-five, both in motion. The sky is clear warm orange and soft blue, first
sunlight falling across the track. In the foreground edge, a schoolbag and a
paper cup of soy milk on the grass. Color palette: warm orange, soft blue,
fresh green. Composition: the two figures small in the middle, lots of sky
negative space for later title text. No text, no logos, no watermark.
```

## 备选方案（极简拍手海报）

```text
Minimalist poster style: two simple hand shapes meeting in a high-five at the
center, drawn as flat silhouettes against a rising sun circle, radiating
concentric warm rings like sound waves. Flat design, warm orange and deep blue,
plenty of negative space, no text.
```

## 禁用元素

- 不出现具体真实人物面孔（防肖像争议）
- 不出现品牌 logo、校名、校徽
- 不要求画中文文字（生成器画字易乱码）
- 不做暗色调伤感风（本歌是明快温暖向）
