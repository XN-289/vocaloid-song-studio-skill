# 《まばたきの外側》封面提示词示例

## Prompt

```text
A square original Japanese vocal-synth narrative single cover. A tired Japanese high-school girl stands alone in a narrow after-school corridor, seen three-quarter from behind, wearing an ordinary dark school uniform with no recognizable copyrighted design. Her reflection in a classroom window has already turned its head toward a thin blue fracture of light in the wall. On the reflection's wrist is a luminous bracelet whose shape can also be read as a plain hospital identification band. Far in the corridor, circular ceiling lights resemble moons, while one silver wheeled silhouette can be interpreted as either a magical staff or an IV stand. The fantasy reading should dominate at first glance; the clinical reading should emerge only after careful viewing. Deep indigo, electric cyan and restrained magenta against institutional white, cinematic anime-inspired digital illustration without imitating a named artist, dramatic rim light, subtle glitch fragments, clean central silhouette, negative space for optional later typography, no generated text, no logo, no watermark.
```

```text
existing anime character, recognizable voicebank mascot, copyrighted costume, named artist imitation, explicit hospital room, visible diagnosis, graphic treatment, self-harm, sexualized minor, fan-service pose, exaggerated breasts, gore, horror face, cluttered composition, generated Japanese text, logo, watermark, malformed hands, extra limbs, low resolution
```

## 设计说明

封面优先显示“魔法觉醒”，只把腕带、轮子和白色走廊藏成第二层。它不会直接剧透病房，也不会复刻任何现有虚拟歌姬或魔法少女角色造型。
